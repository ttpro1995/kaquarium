# Personas

> Upstream: `intent-statement.md` (three audience tiers), confirmed in the user-stories Q&A (Q1).

## Personas

### P1 — The Optimizer (primary)

- **Role**: tycoon / management-sim player.
- **Goals**: build an efficient fish farm, find the best breeding and selling strategies, reach "empire" scale.
- **Pain points**: a dominant single-species strategy makes the game boring; hidden numbers make optimization impossible.
- **Context**: plays in focused sessions, chases the "one more day" loop, reads the market and orders carefully.

### P2 — The Hobbyist (secondary)

- **Role**: real aquarium enthusiast who wants a faithful-but-forgiving simulation.
- **Goals**: keep fish healthy with realistic husbandry, breed fish successfully, learn the nitrogen cycle and compatibility.
- **Pain points**: games that punish mistakes harshly or hide the "why" behind a sick or dead fish.
- **Context**: knows aquarium terms (pH, ammonia, fry); wants depth that respects real aquarium keeping without being punishing.

### P3 — The Cozy Player (secondary)

- **Role**: cozy-game player who wants a relaxing tank to watch and decorate.
- **Goals**: a beautiful, ambient tank; low-stress care; small delightful moments (first fry, a rare colour).
- **Pain points**: timers and penalties that create anxiety; anything that feels like it punishes stepping away.
- **Context**: plays in short, relaxed sessions; cares about aesthetics and calm over optimization.

## Priority

- **P1 (The Optimizer)** is the primary persona — the core loop and economy are optimized for them.
- **P2 (The Hobbyist)** shapes the husbandry depth (water, feeding, breeding) and the "why" tooltips.
- **P3 (The Cozy Player)** shapes the ambient presentation, the forgiving offline behavior, and reduced-motion/accessibility.
