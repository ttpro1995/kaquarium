# Scope Document

> Upstream: `intent-statement.md` (MVP boundary), `feasibility-assessment.md` (feasible; rendering is the top risk), `constraint-register.md` (no integrations or compliance; solo; offline PWA).

## In Scope (MVP)

The MVP delivers the core loop end-to-end — buy fish, feed and care, grow and breed, sell, reinvest.

- One tank with day/night and swimming fish.
- Fish store (data-driven species; buy juveniles).
- Feeding and care (food, satiety, growth).
- Health and death (starvation, unsuitable environment).
- Breeding for the three core archetypes (livebearer, scatter spawner, plant/substrate depositor).
- Eggs, hatching, fry cohorts, first foods.
- Selling (single and bulk) with the price formula and market saturation.
- Basic orders (3–6).
- Progression levels 1–3.
- Autosave and manual save/load; pause and speed controls.

## Out of Scope (deferred)

- Advanced genetics and bloodlines.
- Remaining breeding archetypes (cave, bubble-nester, annual, hard-breed).
- Disease and medicine.
- Full aquascaping and decoration effects.
- Multi-tank farm.

## Value Stream Map

The player's value stream is a closed loop:

```
Observe → Care (feed, fix water) → Grow / Breed → Grade → Sell → Reinvest → (repeat)
```

- Care creates healthy, colourful fish.
- Breeding produces the next generation (fry).
- Selling and orders turn fish into money.
- Reinvestment (food, equipment, species) grows capacity and feeds back into care.

The scarce resources are tank space, water quality, and the player's attention — not money alone.
