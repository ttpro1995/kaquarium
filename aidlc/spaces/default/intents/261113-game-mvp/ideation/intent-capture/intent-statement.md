# Intent Statement

## Problem Statement

The initiative is a browser-based aquarium breeding/management tycoon game. The player's goal is to grow a small home aquarium into a fish empire, pursuing the core fantasy of being "the breeder whose fish everyone wants." [Q1]

## Target Customer

The game serves three audience tiers [Q2]:

- Core tycoon/management-sim players who enjoy optimization loops. [Q2]
- Real aquarium hobbyists who want a faithful-but-forgiving simulation. [Q2]
- Cozy-game players who want a relaxing tank to watch and decorate. [Q2]

## Success Metrics

The MVP succeeds when [Q3]:

- A new player reaches Level 3 in 2 hours ± 30 minutes with no crashes. [Q3][Q9]
- No single-species strategy beats a mixed one by more than ~25% in automated runs. [Q3]
- A 100-fish save runs at ≥ 50 fps on a Meizu Lucky 08 (the reference mid-range phone). [Q3][Q9]

## Initiative Trigger

Build the MVP now to validate the concept before investing further. [Q4]

## Initial Scope Signal

- Workflow-selected scope: `mvp` (workflow-selected) [scope]
- User-confirmed product boundary: ship the core loop — one tank; a store; feeding, growth, health and death; core breeding for the three simplest archetypes; selling with a market; basic orders; levels 1–3; autosave — and deliberately leave out advanced genetics and bloodlines, the remaining breeding archetypes, disease/medicine, full aquascaping, and the multi-tank farm. [Q8]
