### Summary

The commerce design is internally detailed but not yet implementable as written: the notification aggregation splits one decision across a pure mapper and a tick system in a way that double-emits, the bulk/fulfil skip-versus-reject semantics contradict each other, and cross-unit money writes plus a never-awarded feeder unlock break the contracts it claims to respect.
